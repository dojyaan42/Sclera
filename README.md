#  Sclera • Eye Tracking Navigation for Minecraft VR 
Look, aim, and interact with your world naturally.
Sclera brings real-time eye tracking and intuitive gaze controls to Minecraft VR (Visor on Fabric). Target blocks by looking at them, sort inventories with a glance, fire arrows directly where you focus, or rest your arms and let your gaze take over, all seamlessly blended with your physical VR motion controllers.

## Powered by VRCFaceTracking 
Sclera uses the open source VRCFaceTracking (VRCFT) app as its universal eye-tracking bridge.

## Features :

- ***Fluid Hybrid Controls***: Use your eyes and VR controllers together without awkward mode switching. Point with your hands when you want physical action, or rest your arms and let your gaze take over instantly.

- ***Effortless Menu Navigation***: Browse pause menus, settings, and mod screens just by looking. Buttons, toggles, and sliders highlight naturally where your eyes focus without needing to raise your arms.

- ***Gaze Bow Aiming***: Draw your bow, look at your target, and shoot. Eliminates uncomfortable wrist angles while preserving authentic vanilla arrow physics, power, and trajectories.

- ***Magnetic Inventory Navigation***: Glancing across chests and crafting tables is effortless, Sclera gently snaps focus to nearby slots so you don't have to strain your eyes pinpointing tiny boxes.

- ***Action Lock***: Block breaking locks onto your focused block until the action finishes. Hand tremors or trigger clicks will never accidentally reset your mining progress.

- ***Hands Free Blink Actions***: Trigger left-clicks in menus and containers with a quick blink, or optionally place blocks and attack hands free in the 3D world.

- ***Built-in Headset Aim Mode***: Don't have an eye-tracking headset? Switch Aim Source to Head to aim, mine, and browse menus using your headset's center line of sight on any standard VR headset.
